Training

3-day program · 4 exercises per session

About 15–18 minutes per session · dumbbells (5–25kg) + flat bench · arms, chest and shoulders carry 21 of the 36 weekly sets

Day 1

Chest · Back · Triceps · Hinge

  • Neutral-Grip Dumbbell Bench Press — 3 × 6–10Flat bench. Palms facing each other, full range, no overhead lockout. How-to video
  • One-Arm Dumbbell Row — 3 × 8–12 / sideHand and knee on the bench, torso square. Also loads the biceps. How-to video
  • Dumbbell Skull Crusher — 3 × 8–12Flat bench. Triceps in the stretched position, elbows fixed. How-to video
  • Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift — 3 × 8–12Glutes, hamstrings and spinal erectors. Hips back, flat back, dumbbells close to the legs. How-to video
Day 2

Chest · Shoulders · Biceps · Single-Leg

  • Close-Grip Dumbbell Bench Press — 3 × 8–12Flat bench, dumbbells pressed together, elbows tucked. Second triceps exposure of the week. How-to video
  • Dumbbell Lateral Raise — 3 × 12–20Side delts. Slow lowering, no swing. How-to video
  • Seated Dumbbell Curl — 3 × 8–12Biceps. Seated on the flat bench, upper arm still. How-to video
  • Dumbbell Split Squat — 3 × 8–12 / sideSingle-leg glute and quad work — the direct hip-stability lift. Hand on a wall for balance. How-to video
Day 3

Chest · Shoulders · Rear Delts · Biceps

  • Dumbbell Chest Fly — 3 × 10–15Flat bench. Soft elbows, chest stretched at the bottom. How-to video
  • Dumbbell Lateral Raise — 3 × 12–20Second side-delt session of the week. How-to video
  • Seated Bent-Over Dumbbell Reverse Fly — 3 × 10–15Rear delts and upper back — the counterweight to all the pressing. How-to video
  • Dumbbell Hammer Curl — 3 × 8–12Brachialis and forearm, neutral grip throughout. How-to video

Floor block — four exercises, do this anywhere

Two for the hip, one for the shoulder blades, one for mobility. 5 minutes, mat only, aim for 2× per week — no basement space needed.

Nutrition

Meal 1 · Breakfast

Main plate

1 cup (250 g) plain 0% Greek yogurt mixed with 1/2 scoop of whey protein.

Top with 1/2 cup blueberries or raspberries.

Add 1 tbsp pumpkin seeds or crushed walnuts.

No cooking High protein Add healthy fat

Meal 2 · Morning snack

Option A

2 hard-boiled eggs + 1 part-skim string cheese.

Option B

150 g of 2% cottage cheese topped with sliced cucumbers and black pepper.

Meal 3 · Lunch

Main plate

150 g cooked grilled chicken breast or extra-lean ground beef or turkey.

1 cup cooked quinoa or 1 medium baked sweet potato.

2 cups mixed dark leafy greens, plus 1 tbsp olive oil or 1/4 avocado.

Meal 4 · Afternoon snack

Option A

4 slices, about 100 g, of deli smoked turkey breast wrapped around 1/4 avocado.

Option B

120 g plain Greek yogurt mixed with 10–12 almonds.

Meal 5 · Dinner

Rotation A · Omega-3 support

160 g baked salmon fillet with roasted asparagus or Brussels sprouts tossed in olive oil.

Use 3 days/week Omega-3 focus
Rotation B · Iron support

150 g lean sirloin steak or bison with sautéed broccoli and onions.

Add 1 cup brown rice or wild rice.

Use 3 days/week Iron support Pair with tolerated veg
Social / alcohol day rule

Drop the rice or potatoes, but keep the protein portion and vegetables the same.

Meal 6 · Night snack

Option A

150 g plain cottage cheese dusted with cinnamon.

Option B

1 scoop micellar casein protein powder blended with water and 1 tbsp chia seeds.

Rule

Keep this low-sugar and protein-centered.

Most useful lab strategy

  • Keep the food pattern simple enough to repeat for at least 6–8 weeks before judging whether nutrition changes are working.
  • Use omega-3, ferritin context, and CBC trend as the main improve / monitor area.
  • Leave the already-good markers alone unless they start moving the wrong way.

Best repeat-check priorities

  • Repeat lipids and omega-3 after a period of actually consistent intake.
  • Repeat ferritin and CBC together if symptoms, energy, or recovery are still in question.
  • Use trends, not single numbers, as the decision driver.
