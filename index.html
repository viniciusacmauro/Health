Training Nutrition Routine

Training

3-day program · 4 exercises per session

About 15–18 minutes per session with 45 s rests · dumbbells (5–25kg) + flat bench · arms, chest and shoulders carry 27 of the 36 weekly sets

Day 1

Chest · Back · Triceps · Hinge

  • Neutral-Grip Dumbbell Bench Press — 3 × 6–10Flat bench. Palms facing each other, full range, no overhead lockout. How-to video
  • One-Arm Dumbbell Row — 3 × 8–12 / sideHand and knee on the bench, torso square. Also loads the biceps. How-to video
  • Dumbbell Skull Crusher — 3 × 8–12Flat bench. Triceps in the stretched position, elbows fixed. How-to video
  • Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift — 3 × 8–12Glutes, hamstrings and spinal erectors. Hips back, flat back, dumbbells close to the legs. How-to video
Day 2

Chest · Shoulders · Biceps · Single-Leg

  • Close-Grip Dumbbell Bench Press — 3 × 8–12Flat bench, dumbbells pressed together, elbows tucked. Second triceps exposure of the week. How-to video
  • Dumbbell Lateral Raise — 3 × 12–20Side delts. Slow lowering, no swing. How-to video
  • Seated Dumbbell Curl — 3 × 8–12Biceps. Seated on the flat bench, upper arm still. How-to video
  • Dumbbell Split Squat — 3 × 8–12 / sideSingle-leg glute and quad work — the direct hip-stability lift. Hand on a wall for balance. How-to video
Day 3

Chest · Shoulders · Rear Delts · Biceps

  • Dumbbell Chest Fly — 3 × 10–15Flat bench. Soft elbows, chest stretched at the bottom. How-to video
  • Dumbbell Lateral Raise — 3 × 12–20Second side-delt session of the week. How-to video
  • Seated Bent-Over Dumbbell Reverse Fly — 3 × 10–15Rear delts and upper back — the counterweight to all the pressing. How-to video
  • Dumbbell Hammer Curl — 3 × 8–12Brachialis and forearm, neutral grip throughout. How-to video

Floor block — four exercises, do this anywhere

Two for the hip, one for the shoulder blades, one for mobility. About 5–7 minutes, mat only, aim for 2× per week — no basement space needed.

  • Side Plank with Hip Abduction — 2 × 8–12 / sideHighest gluteus medius activation measured (about 103% MVIC, Boren 2011). From a forearm side plank, raise the top leg without letting the hips drop. How-to video
  • Side Plank — 2 × 15–30 sec / sideTrunk and lateral hip together. Equal time both sides. How-to video
  • Scapular Push-Up / Push-Up Plus — 2 × 10–15Serratus and scapular-control work without the neck-painful prone Y position. How-to video
  • Half-Kneeling Hip Flexor Stretch — 1 × 30 sec / sideRear knee down, squeeze that glute, tuck the pelvis. How-to video

Nutrition

Meal 1 · Breakfast

Option A · Greek yogurt bowlNo cooking · ~40 g protein

250 g plain 0% Greek yogurt mixed with 1/2 scoop of whey protein.

Top with 1/2 cup blueberries or raspberries.

Add 1 tbsp pumpkin seeds or crushed walnuts.

No cookingHigh protein
Option B · Egg scrambleCholine-focused · ~30 g protein

3 whole eggs plus 2 egg whites, scrambled with a large handful of spinach.

1/4 avocado and 1 slice of rye or sourdough on the side.

CookedCholine
Option C · Protein oatsTraining mornings · ~35 g protein

50 g rolled oats cooked in water or milk.

Stir in 1 scoop of whey after it cools slightly, so it doesn't clump.

Half a banana and 1 tbsp natural peanut butter.

Carb-forwardPre-training

Meal 3 · Lunch

Option A · Chicken and quinoa~50 g protein

150 g cooked grilled chicken breast, extra-lean ground beef or turkey.

1 cup cooked quinoa or 1 medium baked sweet potato.

2 cups mixed dark leafy greens with 1 tbsp olive oil or 1/4 avocado.

Balanced
Option B · Sardines or tunaOmega-3 · ~35 g protein

1 tin (100–120 g) of sardines or tuna on 2 slices of rye, with tomato and olive oil.

Side salad of greens.

Your omega-3 index was 5.91%, below the ≥8% target. Sardines are the cheapest way to move it. Canned tuna has a fraction of the EPA and DHA, so on tuna days the omega-3 comes from salmon and fish oil instead.

Omega-3 (sardines)No cooking
Option C · Beef and rice bowlIron · ~50 g protein

150 g extra-lean ground beef with 1 cup cooked rice.

Half a cup of black beans, salsa, and a handful of greens.

Heme iron plus the beans' folate; useful given ferritin at 97 µg/L.
